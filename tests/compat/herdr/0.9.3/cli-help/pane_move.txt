Move a pane

Usage: herdr pane move [OPTIONS] <PANE_ID>

Arguments:
  <PANE_ID>
          

Options:
      --tab <TAB_ID>
          

      --split <DIRECTION>
          [possible values: right, down]

      --target-pane <ID>
          

      --ratio <FLOAT>
          

      --new-tab
          

      --workspace <ID>
          

      --new-workspace
          

      --label <TEXT>
          

      --tab-label <TEXT>
          

      --focus
          

      --no-focus
